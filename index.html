Of course the story doesn't end at the tram stop. Iva packed her bags two weeks later and got on a plane to her sweetest boy in Sofia. Simo picked her up at the airport, as he couldn't wait any longer to kiss her pretty lips again.

She was supposed to be home an hour later, but they sat outside her place for too long and her mother was getting uppity. For the few days they had together, they were constantly being pulled away by work or friends or relatives.

And though they spent too much time just hugging on the sofa at Iva's place, it never seemed enough. There was a single day where they didn't need to text each other a 'good morning' and 'good night'. Simo didn't know it yet, but that's all he needed to have a perfect day.
